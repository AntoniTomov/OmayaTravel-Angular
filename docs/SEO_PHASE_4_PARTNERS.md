## Material to assemble before a first approach

1. A named Omaya spokesperson and a short, verifiable biography; a named guide or local contributor for the chosen story.
2. Three to five original photographs with captions, creator credits and confirmed permission for the proposed use. Existing website files alone do not establish permission to redistribute them to publishers.

5. For commercial distribution: live dates and capacity, net/retail rates, settlement and cancellation terms, emergency support, supplier responsibilities, actual insurance documents and evidence of relevant operating practices.

This is an asset list, not a request to delay preparation. Story angles, product links and the brief above can be developed now. Toni has deferred rooming, supplements and fitness information for a few days.

## How to assess a proposal

Record the publication/partner, named contact, format, relevant country/product, current audience evidence, cash cost, hosted-place cost, commission, staff time, agreed deliverables and timing. For earned coverage, the editor retains the decision about publication and links. Treat sponsored coverage and commercial distribution as paid acquisition with their own costs and disclosures; do not buy placement for a promised ranking benefit.

Measure qualified referral enquiries and resulting bookings, alongside relevant exposure. Keep B2B leads separate from leisure enquiries, and record how the traveller heard of Omaya when analytics cannot attribute the visit. Compare contribution after fees and hosting costs before repeating a paid trial. A link count or a publisher's historical audience headline is insufficient evidence of commercial success.

For a first batch, prepare two tailored approaches and one trade proposal, then seek explicit instruction to send them. The initial research is complete; contacts, audience figures, application availability and prices should be rechecked on the actual outreach date.
