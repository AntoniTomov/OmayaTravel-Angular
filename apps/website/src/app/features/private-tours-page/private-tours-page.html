What Is a Private Tour?

A private tour is travel designed entirely around you. At Omaya Travel, our team creates personalised itineraries built from scratch — your dates, your pace, your interests.

Why settle for a group tour filled with stops that don’t excite you? Maybe you’ve found an itinerary that’s almost right, but not quite. Or perhaps you have a vision in mind and just need the right people to bring it to life.

That’s where we come in. Our experts handle every detail, so all you have to do is show up and enjoy the journey.
